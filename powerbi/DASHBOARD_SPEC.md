# Dashboard specification

Pages: Executive Experience Health; Why Customers Become Dissatisfied; Customer Journey Friction; Promise and Waiting Behaviour; Intervention and Recovery; Operational Insights; Business Recommendations. Use KPI cards for CSAT, DSAT, NPS, response, resolution, silent wait, effort, risk, and promise reliability. Use bar charts by channel, industry, issue, transfer band, wait band, and promise status; use a line chart for case volume and CSAT over time. Every page should carry date, industry, channel, issue, priority, sentiment, and risk slicers.
