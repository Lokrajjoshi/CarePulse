# CarePulse Business Value

CarePulse is an experience intelligence layer for support operations. It reads structured case events and approved conversation signals, then turns them into a shared early-warning and action system.

## Why it is different

An agent can notice a late case. CarePulse makes that knowledge consistent across thousands of cases, channels, teams, and time periods. It measures silent wait, missed promises, repeated explanations, transfers, sentiment movement, and customer effort together; explains the risk score; recommends an intervention; and preserves the evidence for operations and leadership.

## Who benefits

- Agents see the next best action before frustration becomes a survey result.
- Team leads see which queues, promises, and processes create avoidable DSAT.
- Business leaders connect service friction to recovery opportunity and operational priorities.
- Customers receive clearer updates and fewer repeated explanations.

## Interview summary

“I built CarePulse as a privacy-aware, event-driven customer experience intelligence platform. It does not replace the CRM or helpdesk. It connects to approved case events, derives explainable risk and sentiment locally, and gives agents and leaders one operational view of where experience is deteriorating and what to do next.”
