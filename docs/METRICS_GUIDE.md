# Metrics guide

Silent wait is the longest interval between case creation, meaningful updates, or resolution. Customer effort is `1 + 2*transfers + 2*repeated_explanations + 3*repeat_contact + 3*missed_promises + min(silent_wait_hours, 8)`, capped at 20. Promise reliability is met promises divided by all promises. CSAT is mean feedback score; DSAT is the share with score 1 or 2; NPS is promoters (9-10) minus detractors (0-6). Every measure is descriptive and limited by the synthetic data design.
