# Role-Based Demo Script

Use this guide when showing CarePulse to a lecturer, recruiter, or project reviewer. It follows the same support case through the roles that need to act on it.

## Scenario

A customer has a technical issue that has taken longer than expected. The case has moved between teams, a promised update may be due or missed, and the customer needs a clear next step.

The goal of the demonstration is to show how CarePulse helps a team review the operational journey and coordinate recovery. The demo does not claim to measure a customer's feelings.

## 1. Executive or business manager: find the priority

Open the executive dashboard.

Show:

- the overall case workload;
- the high-risk count;
- the link from the high-risk summary to the risk monitor.

Explain:

> A manager first needs to know where attention is most needed. CarePulse uses recorded operational signals to create a review queue, not an automatic decision about the customer.

## 2. CX manager: review the risk queue

Open the risk monitor and select a high-priority case.

Show:

- the case owner;
- the risk-band explanation and its visible drivers;
- promise state and missed-commitment information;
- available feedback, while noting that no feedback is different from negative feedback.

Explain:

> The manager can see why a case needs review before assigning an action. This supports a consistent discussion of waiting, commitments, and ownership.

## 3. Agent: inspect the journey and record recovery

Open the agent workspace or case page.

Show:

- the case timeline;
- interactions and transfer history;
- the current owner;
- recovery actions with status, outcome, and follow-up note.

Explain:

> The risk signal becomes useful only when it leads to an accountable action. The agent can record what was done, who owns it, and what should happen next.

## 4. Administrator: keep rules reviewable

Open organisation settings.

Show:

- the configurable medium and high risk thresholds;
- that thresholds can be reviewed instead of being hidden in the source code.

Explain:

> Different organisations may review risk differently. Making thresholds visible keeps the demo rule-based and reviewable.

## 5. Customer view: show clear status

Open the customer-facing case view.

Show:

- current case status;
- relevant updates or promises;
- the information a customer can use to understand the next step.

Explain:

> A good support experience is not only a resolved technical issue. It also needs clear communication about what is happening and what comes next.

## Questions a reviewer can ask

- Which operational signals made this case worth reviewing?
- What is the next recovery action, and who owns it?
- Which part of the case is a recorded fact, and which part is a rule-based interpretation?
- What would need to change before this could use real customer data?

## Honest boundary

All records in this demonstration are synthetic. CarePulse is a prototype for reviewing support journeys and coordinating recovery work. It is not a deployed customer-support service and should not be presented as proof of real customer outcomes.
