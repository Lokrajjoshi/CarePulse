THE DIFFERENTIATOR

It is not another case screen. It is the layer between noticing and improving.

NORMAL CASE HANDLING“I can see this case is delayed.”

The signal is visible only to the person already looking at that case. The response depends on memory, workload, and individual judgment.

  • One case at a time
  • Reactive after the problem is visible
  • Action is not consistently recorded
  • Learning stays inside the case
→
WITH CAREPULSE“I know which cases need attention, why, what to do, and what the organization should fix.”

CarePulse connects scattered signals across journeys and turns them into a prioritized, explainable decision.

  • Prioritizes across the queue
  • Detects deterioration before final DSAT
  • Recommends a reasoned care action
  • Aggregates cases into process learning
In one sentence:Agents can notice friction. CarePulse makes friction measurable, prioritizable, actionable, and learnable at scale.
