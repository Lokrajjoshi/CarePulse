FINAL PROJECT BLUEPRINT

CarePulse, clearly defined.

An interview-ready view of the problem, the users, the architecture, the technology, and the path from local prototype to private organizational deployment.

One-line definitionCarePulse is an explainable customer-experience intelligence layer that sits beside existing support systems and turns live journey signals into prioritized care actions and business learning.
THE PROBLEM

Organizations learn too late.

CSAT and DSAT arrive after the interaction. By then, the opportunity to communicate, clarify ownership, or recover the customer may have passed.

THE SOLUTION

Detect, decide, improve.

CarePulse detects experience deterioration, explains the risk, recommends a next step, and aggregates repeated friction for management.

WHO BENEFITS
AgentPrioritized queue and explainable next action.
CustomerClearer ownership, updates, and options.
BusinessProcess patterns and measurable improvement.
WHAT IT IS NOT

Not a replacement CRM.

It is not a chatbot, ticketing system, or public AI upload tool. It is a private intelligence layer that can connect to approved internal data.
