# Excel learning guide

Open `data/exports/cases_analysis.csv` in Excel. Turn it into a table, filter `risk_band`, use `COUNTIF` for HIGH cases, `AVERAGEIF` for CSAT by channel, `XLOOKUP` to bring customer attributes into a case view, and build PivotTables/charts by industry, issue, promise, and DSAT. Run `python analytics\python\export_data.py` after a data reset to refresh the CSV.
